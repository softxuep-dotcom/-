# Fling Fiasco — Store Listing Draft

## Title

Fling Fiasco

## Short description

Fling a floppy toy hero through tiny 3D stunt sets and ring the bell.

## Long description

The toybox curtain is up, the bell is waiting, and your performer has absolutely
no dignity to lose. Swipe to launch a fully jointed ragdoll through bumpers,
fans, springs, crates, crushers, and moving platforms. Read each miniature
stage, choose a route, and try to finish with fewer flings and a faster time.

Fling Fiasco contains 12 handcrafted, replayable stunt stages. Each short round
feeds directly into a clear score, stars, new unlocks, and an instant retry.
Its portrait-first layout is designed for one-finger phone play while retaining
the same gesture on mouse and optional keyboard controls on desktop.

## Controls

- Touch or mouse: drag toward the bell and release to fling.
- Keyboard: arrow keys or WASD aim; Space/Enter fling; R retries; P/Escape pauses.
- Menus: touch, mouse, keyboard focus, and Enter/Space are supported.
